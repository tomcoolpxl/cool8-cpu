# DawnLike — MOTT's tiles

**DawnLike, 16x16 Universal Rogue-like tileset v1.81, by
DragonDePlatino, on DawnBringer's 16-colour palette.** Licensed
[CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Downloaded
from [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/dawnlike-16x16-universal-rogue-like-tileset-v181)
(`DawnLike_5.zip`, 1,008,505 bytes) on 13 September 2026.

The author's own terms are in [`README.txt`](README.txt): credit
**DawnBringer**, whose palette the pack lives on, and hide the Platino
sprite from `Characters/Reptile0.png` somewhere in the game. In MOTT he
appears only beside a hero who writes "Platino" in the dust.

`tools/mkmott.py` cuts `demos/mott.act`'s tiles from these sheets
into `assets/mott/`; the only change it makes to a pixel is the one
the machine makes, 24-bit colour to 12. The credit is in
[docs/14-demos.md](../../docs/14-demos.md).

What is here is the pack's `Characters/`, `Items/`, `Objects/` and
`GUI/` (including the `SDS_8x8.ttf` font MOTT's text is in) and its
`README.txt`; the `Examples/` and `Commissions/` folders were left out.
